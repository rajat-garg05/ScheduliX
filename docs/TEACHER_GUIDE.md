# ScheduliX — Teacher Guide

This guide explains how teachers sign in, view schedules, track daily substitutes, and request leave in **ScheduliX**.

---

## 1. First login and password setup
Teachers do not register separately. Your HOD adds you under Setup, which creates your account with:
- **Email:** your official email address
- **Initial password:** `ScheduliX@123`

On your first login, **ScheduliX** sends you directly to **Change password**:
1. Enter `ScheduliX@123` as **Current password**.
2. Choose a new password (at least 10 characters), confirm it, and click **Update password**.
3. You will then land on your **Dashboard**.

If you ever forget your password later, click **Forgot password?** on the login screen to receive a reset link valid for 30 minutes (or ask your HOD to reset your password to the default).

---

## 2. Dashboard (**Dashboard**)
Your Dashboard highlights:
- **My lectures today** (with substitute details if someone is covering for you or you are covering another class)
- Latest unread notifications
- Quick links to your timetable and leave requests

---

## 3. Viewing timetables
- **My timetable:** Lists your regular weekly lectures across all saved class timetables, plus any upcoming substitute arrangements where you are covering for another teacher or another teacher is covering for you.
- **All timetables:** Read-only view of all saved section timetables in your department. Use search or pick a section to inspect its full weekly grid.
- **Today:** Shows every section's lectures for the selected date (defaults to today). Confirmed substitutes appear clearly with a **Substitute** mark.

---

## 4. Asking for leave (**Leave**)
1. Open **Leave** from the left menu and click **Ask for leave**.
2. Pick **From date** and **To date** (today or future dates) and add an optional **Reason**.
3. Click **Send request**.
4. Your request stays in **Waiting for HOD** status (you can click **Cancel** while it is still waiting).
5. Once your HOD accepts or rejects it, you receive a notification under the **bell icon** in the top bar.

---

## 5. Notifications & Profile menu
- Click the **bell icon** in the top bar to view unread and past alerts (leave decisions, substitute assignments, or password changes).
- Click your name in the top-right corner anytime to **Change password** or **Log out**.
